What this website keeps about you

Last updated 6 November 2026

In short

This website has no cookies, no adverts and no tracking. We don’t count or follow visitors. We only learn anything about you if you write to us.

What your browser keeps

To save you choosing again on your next visit, your own browser keeps two things, in its local storage:

  • The city you picked for the prayer times.
  • How far you watched each film, so it can carry on from where you stopped.

These stay on your device and are never sent to us. To remove them, clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings.

To choose a city near you the first time, the page reads your device’s time zone. This also stays in your browser.

When you write to us

The contact form doesn’t send anything by itself. It opens your own email app with your message written out, and nothing reaches us until you press send there. When you do, we receive what you wrote: your name, your email address, your WhatsApp number if you gave it, and your message.

We use these only to reply to you. We won’t publish your name or anything you send without asking first. To ask us to delete your messages, write to isnadmedia9@gmail.com.

Other services

  • Fonts. The lettering on this site comes from Google Fonts, so your browser fetches it from Google, which sees your IP address as it would for any website. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Hosting. Like every website, ours is kept on a web host, which may keep standard records of visits, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Links. Our links to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

The films and pictures are kept on this site itself; watching them doesn’t involve anyone else.
